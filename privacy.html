Privacy Policy

Learn Mirpuri & Pothwari for Windows. Effective 19 November 2026. Operator: MD Shahariar Nazim Joy. Contact: dmjks120@gmail.com

Learn Mirpuri & Pothwari does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or accounts.

What stays on your device

Onboarding answers (goal, label, teaching path, optional region affinity, reading preference, daily minutes), heard phrases, saved phrases, quiz results and practice days are stored in the app's local folder on your PC. They are not uploaded to a developer server. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Audio

All practice audio is bundled with the app and plays offline. The Windows app does not use the microphone.

Purchases

Monthly Access and Lifetime Unlock are processed by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles your payment details and account. The developer does not receive your payment card details. The app asks the Microsoft Store only whether your add-on licence is active.

No accounts, ads, or third-party analytics

There is no sign-in. There are no ads. There is no third-party analytics SDK. Data is not sold and is not shared with data brokers.

Outside links

Links to further resources, this policy and support open in your web browser. Those sites have their own policies.

Children

This app is a language companion for families, including adult learners. It is not directed at children.
